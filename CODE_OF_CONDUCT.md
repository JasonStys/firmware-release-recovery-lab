# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Contributors are expected to make participation respectful, harassment-free, and welcoming
regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific, constructive, and kind in technical discussion.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Respect safety boundaries and confidentiality.
- Acknowledge mistakes and help repair their impact.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of
secrets, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Maintainers may edit,
remove, or reject contributions and restrict participation when necessary. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as practical.
